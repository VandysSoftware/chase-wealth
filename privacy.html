1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy covers information collected through the Chase Wealth Management website at chasewm.com. It does not cover information you provide to us offline, by phone, or in the course of an advisory relationship.

Chase Wealth Management is a state-registered investment adviser. If you become a client, the firm also provides a separate privacy notice describing how it handles your nonpublic personal information under applicable financial-privacy law. That notice, not this page, governs client information; see section 12.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

If you use the contact form on our site, we collect the information you enter (your name, email address, and the message and topic you provide) so we can respond to your inquiry.

If you schedule a meeting, the scheduling is handled by our third-party scheduling tool (Calendly). The name, email, and any notes you enter there are provided directly to that tool, not stored by this website. See section 4.

Please do not include account numbers, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive personal information in the contact form or scheduling notes.

Information collected automatically

Our website is served by a third-party hosting provider (GitHub Pages). Like most web servers, it may automatically log basic technical information when you visit (such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request, and the date and time of your visit) for security and reliability.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests those font files from Google's servers, which involves sharing your IP address with Google for that purpose.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies, and it uses no analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking technologies. If you open the embedded scheduling tool, that third-party tool may set its own cookies within its frame, governed by its own privacy policy.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • respond to your inquiries and communicate with you;
  • schedule and hold meetings you request;
  • operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website;
  • comply with our legal, regulatory, and recordkeeping obligations.

We do not use website information to make automated decisions about you, and we do not sell it.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers that operate this site and its tools, and only as needed to provide their service:
    • Calendly: meeting scheduling (privacy policy);
    • Google: delivery of Google Fonts (privacy policy);
    • GitHub: website hosting via GitHub Pages (privacy statement);
    • [Web3Forms or the chosen contact-form provider]: delivery of contact-form submissions, if the form is enabled.
  • Legal and safety: when required by law, regulation, subpoena, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of the firm, our clients, or others.

5. Data retention

We keep inquiry and scheduling information for as long as needed to respond to you and to meet the firm's legal and regulatory recordkeeping obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized. Information held by our service providers is retained according to their own policies.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information handled through this site. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. As noted above, please do not send sensitive personal or account information through the website.

7. Your choices and rights

You can choose not to provide information through the site, though some features (such as the contact form or scheduling) may not work without it. You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to request its deletion, and to ask us not to contact you further.

Depending on the state you live in, you may have additional rights regarding your personal information. To exercise any of these, contact us using section 11.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Links to other sites

Our site links to third-party websites, for example, the adviser's regulatory record on the SEC's Investment Adviser Public Disclosure site, and the scheduling tool. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and this policy does not apply to them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here.
